### 2. Huawei Petal Ads SDK & Open Advertising ID (OAID) Disclosure
To keep this utility free for all users on **Huawei AppGallery**, the application integrates the official **Huawei Petal Ads SDK (`com.huawei.hms:ads-lite` / `huawei_ads`)** operated by Huawei Services.
- **Data Processed by Petal Ads SDK:**
  - **Open Advertising ID (`OAID`)** for frequency capping, attribution, and optional personalized or non-personalized advertising.
  - **Basic Device & Network Metadata:** device model, OS version, screen dimensions, system language/locale, and network connection type (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`).
  - **Ad Interaction Events:** banner impressions, interstitial ad displays, clicks, and anti-fraud diagnostics.
- **Developer Server Storage:** **Nguyen Minh Thanh** operates **no proprietary backend database** and does **not** store, sell, or profile personal identity information (`name`, `phone number`, `contacts`, `photos`, or `precise GPS location`).

### 3. Audio Output Only — Strict Zero Microphone Access Guarantee
- **Audio Output Only (`STREAM_MUSIC`):** All cleaning frequencies (`165Hz`, `100Hz–200Hz` frequency sweep, pulse waves) and speaker diagnostic tones (`Bass`, `Treble`, `Beep`) are synthesized locally in memory and played exclusively out through your device's speaker hardware.
- **Zero Recording / Zero Microphone Access:** This application **DOES NOT declare `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`**, **NEVER accesses your device's Microphone**, and **NEVER records, listens to, or transmits ambient sound or voice data** under any circumstance.

### 4. Hardware & Liquid Ingress Disclaimer
- **Auxiliary Utility Only:** Acoustic sound waves help dislodge surface water droplets trapped on external speaker meshes using air pressure excursion. They **cannot** evaporate moisture inside sealed internal circuitry or repair corroded components.
- **Limitation of Liability:** You use high-volume vibration modes at your own discretion. **Nguyen Minh Thanh** shall not be held liable for any direct, indirect, or consequential hardware damage resulting from pre-existing liquid immersion, internal short-circuits, worn speaker membranes, or improper handling of wet devices. Power off and consult an authorized service center immediately if liquid has penetrated internal electronics.

### 5. User Data Subject Rights & How to Opt-Out / Reset OAID
You retain full control over your privacy and advertising preferences at all times:
1. **Opt Out of Personalized Ads / Reset OAID on Huawei (HarmonyOS / EMUI):**
   - Go to **Settings** → **Privacy** → **Ads and privacy** (or **HMS Core** → **Ads**).
   - Enable **Limit ad tracking** to disable personalized ads, and/or tap **Reset Ad-ID** to generate a brand-new anonymous **OAID** immediately.
2. **Standard Android Devices:**
   - Go to **Settings** → **Privacy** → **Ads** → **Delete advertising ID** or **Reset advertising ID**.
3. **Access, Inquiry & Deletion Requests:**
   - Because the developer stores zero personal data on external servers, uninstalling the app immediately removes all local app state from your device. For any privacy rights inquiry or assistance with third-party ad partner data requests, email **[alphaplus.thanh82@gmail.com](mailto:alphaplus.thanh82@gmail.com)** — we respond within **15 business days**.
