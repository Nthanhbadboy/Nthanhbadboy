2. Huawei Petal Ads SDK & OAID Collection

This app integrates the official Huawei Petal Ads SDK (huawei_ads / com.huawei.hms:ads-lite) to display Banner Ads at the bottom of the main screen and Interstitial Ads upon completion of a speaker cleaning cycle.

  • Identifiers Collected by Petal Ads SDK: Open Advertising ID (OAID), basic device model/screen parameters, system language, network state, and ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, anti-fraud checks).
  • No Proprietary Personal Profiling: The developer maintains no backend server storing your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or GPS coordinates.

3. Audio Output Only — Strictly NO Microphone Recording

We strictly use Audio Output (STREAM_MUSIC) ONLY to emit synthetic 165Hz and 100Hz–200Hz acoustic waves to clean water droplets from your speaker grille, as well as test tones (Bass, Treble, Beep).

Guarantee: This application does NOT request RECORD_AUDIO permission, NEVER accesses your Microphone, and NEVER records or transmits audio.

4. Hardware & Liquid Damage Disclaimer

Acoustic vibration assists in expelling surface moisture from speaker grilles and is not a substitute for professional hardware repair. Nguyen Minh Thanh disclaims any liability for pre-existing water ingress, internal motherboard corrosion, short circuits, or hardware damage resulting from liquid exposure.

5. User Data Subject Rights & Opt-Out of OAID

  • Huawei / HarmonyOS / EMUI Devices: Open Settings → Privacy → Ads and privacy, enable Limit ad tracking, or tap Reset Ad-ID to reset your OAID immediately.
  • Android Devices: Open Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Data Inquiries & Deletion: Uninstalling the app deletes all local state immediately. You may also contact alphaplus.thanh82@gmail.com at any time (response within 15 business days).
