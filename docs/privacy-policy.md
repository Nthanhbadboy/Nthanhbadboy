# Privacy Policy — One-Line Physics Draw

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **One-Line Physics Draw** (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by the developer of the [Nthanhbadboy/Nthanhbadboy repository](https://github.com/Nthanhbadboy/Nthanhbadboy).

## Information we collect

The current release does not require an account and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, payment credentials, or advertising identifiers.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores game preferences and progress locally on your device, including level progress, stars, hints, language, sound preference, and game settings. This information is stored using WebView/browser local storage and is not sent to our servers.

You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.

## Ads and in-app purchases

Ads and in-app purchases are disabled in the current release. The Huawei APK links the official HMS Core IAP SDK only for AppGallery compatibility; it does not query products, open checkout, process payments, create orders, or grant entitlements in this release. Any future release that enables paid features will update this policy and use the applicable official store disclosures and receipt verification.

## Third-party resources

The game may request technical resources from third-party content delivery services, including Matter.js from cdnjs, Font Awesome from cdnjs, and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive ordinary technical request information such as an IP address and browser/device request metadata when the resource is downloaded. The App does not send game progress or account data to those services.

The Huawei Android flavor also contains the official HMS Core IAP SDK from Huawei Software Technologies Co., Ltd. It is linked for AppGallery integration, but the current free release does not query products, open a checkout screen, or send a transaction. If paid features are enabled in a future release, the SDK may process transaction information to deliver Huawei payment services and this policy will be updated with the active data flow.

For Android builds, network access is used only for these game resources and official Huawei services while monetization is disabled.

## Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information in the current release, there is no registration process for children.

## Privacy rights

Because the current release does not maintain a server-side personal-data account, there is no personal profile for us to export or delete. You can delete locally stored game data by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App.

For questions or privacy requests, contact the developer through the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/Nthanhbadboy/Nthanhbadboy/issues).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
